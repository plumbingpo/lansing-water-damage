Every water damage restoration job starts with extraction, and no other early step matters more. The faster standing water is removed from your property, the less damage occurs to structural materials, flooring, personal belongings and building systems. Every hour that water sits on surfaces, it penetrates deeper into materials, soaking through carpet into padding, wicking up drywall, saturating wood framing and seeping into subflooring. Lansing Restoration provides emergency water extraction throughout Lansing using industrial-grade truck-mounted and portable equipment capable of removing hundreds of gallons per minute.

Truck-mounted units rank with the strongest water removal systems made, and their suction lifts water out of hard floors, carpet and structural materials much better than anything you can rent or buy. Confined spaces, wall cavities and the gaps between floor layers call for different gear, so we use portable extractors, specialty tools and submersible pumps in places the truck-mounted hoses cannot get to.

There is more to a professional extraction than getting rid of the water in plain sight. Using moisture detection equipment, our IICRC-certified team finds water that has moved out of sight, whether under cabinets, beneath flooring, behind baseboards or inside walls. The more moisture our specialty tools take from these concealed areas ahead of drying, the shorter and lighter the structural drying job becomes, and the lower the chance of secondary mold growth.

How to Prevent Water Extraction Problems

Taking preventive measures can help you avoid costly water damage issues down the road. Follow these tips from our IICRC-certified technicians:

  • Keep a basic emergency water kit: old towels, a bucket and your restoration company's number
  • Critical areas of the house should each have a leak sensor that you know is working
  • Shelving in low areas keeps belongings out of the water if the floor floods
  • Add our number, (442) 207-6150, to your cell so help is one tap away in a water emergency
  • Maintain appliance supply lines and replace hoses that show wear or age
  • Consider an automatic shutoff valve, which senses a leak and closes the water supply

Signs You Need Water Extraction in Lansing

Recognizing the warning signs early can save you time, money, and prevent significant damage to your property. Here are the most common indicators that it's time to call a professional:

  • Floors flooded by a fire sprinkler or suppression system going off
  • Active leaks from plumbing, appliances or HVAC systems
  • Flooded window wells or stairwells letting water into the building
  • Wet baseboards and water creeping from beneath kitchen or bath cabinets
  • A utility area, mechanical room or crawl space with water in it
  • Water squeezing up between floorboards, planks or tiles when you walk
  • Water sitting on a floor anywhere in the building, no matter where it came from

What a Professional Brings to Water Extraction

Both the harm done to your home and the restoration bill depend directly on quick, thorough extraction.
