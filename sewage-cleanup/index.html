Few emergencies in a building are as hazardous as sewage backing up into it. Anyone who comes in contact with contaminated material is exposed to parasites, bacteria and viruses, among them giardia, E. coli, salmonella and hepatitis, all carried in raw sewage and all dangerous. Across Lansing, Lansing Restoration answers sewage emergencies 24/7, sending trained technicians who arrive with EPA-registered disinfectants and the right protective equipment so your property is decontaminated safely.

Sewage damage is classified as Category 3 (black water), the most contaminated and dangerous category of water damage. Because of it, cleanup follows the toughest rules in the trade, which means full protective gear, barriers to contain the area, tearing out any porous material that met sewage and disinfecting all that is left. Our technicians follow IICRC S500 standards for Category 3 water damage so your property is restored to safe occupancy standards.

Here is what the work covers: standing sewage is pumped out, contaminated water is extracted from the structure, porous materials the sewage touched (drywall, insulation, carpet and padding) are torn out and disposed of properly, hard surfaces are disinfected thoroughly, antimicrobials are applied, and the structure is dried so mold does not follow. To finish, thermal foggers and hydroxyl generators of industrial grade deodorize the space so the sewage smell does not linger.

Maintenance Tips for Sewage Cleanup

A little prevention goes a long way in avoiding expensive restoration emergencies. Here are our expert recommendations:

  • Find your sewer cleanout now and check its condition, so it can be reached in an emergency
  • Do not wait on a slow drain, which can turn into a full blockage
  • Toilet paper is the only thing to flush, and that rules out wipes sold as flushable, which still clog
  • Use drain strainers in showers and sinks to catch hair and debris
  • Route sump pump and gutter water away from the sewer line, which can back up when storm flow overloads it
  • Protect against municipal sewer backups by having a backwater valve fitted where your line connects

Common Sewage Cleanup Questions

Some things can be kept and others cannot. We can generally clean and disinfect non-porous belongings, for example glass, metal, hard plastic and tile that is sealed. Upholstery, mattresses, carpet with its pad, insulation and drywall soak up sewage and cannot be made safe, so they are thrown out. Each discarded item is documented, giving you a record to use with your insurer.
Removing the sewage and decontaminating the property is usually done in 1 to 3 days. How extensive the damage is determines the rest, and restoring everything with new materials may need 1 to 3 weeks. Our first priority is making the property safe to occupy again as fast as we can.
A blocked sewer line on the property is the most common starting point. Grease buildup, wipes and other items that should not be flushed, and tree roots growing into the pipe are the usual causes. Sometimes an aging pipe cracks or caves in, or a storm overwhelms the city sewer and forces sewage up the floor drains. To know for certain, have the line camera-inspected once cleanup is finished.

A small backup that stayed contained might be $1,500 to $3,000, but widespread sewage flooding can pass $15,000 once replacement materials and reconstruction are counted.

Why Hire a Professional for Sewage Cleanup?

This is one cleanup you should never take on yourself. The biological hazards in raw sewage require specialized training, proper personal protective equipment (Tyvek suits, respirators, protective gloves and boots) and EPA-registered disinfection products that are not available to consumers. People exposed to the pathogens in sewage can become seriously ill with skin and respiratory infections, hepatitis or gastrointestinal disease.

Disposal matters as well, since everything the sewage contaminated has to be handled as biohazard waste. It is illegal to throw sewage-contaminated insulation, drywall or carpet in the regular trash, and doing so puts the community and the workers who collect it at risk. Certified restoration companies maintain proper waste disposal protocols and relationships with licensed biohazard waste disposal facilities.

Warning Signs for Sewage Cleanup

Catching these issues early helps you avoid costly problems and protect your property. Here are the most common indicators that it's time to call a professional:

  • Soggy, smelly ground over the sewer line or leakage at the cleanout cap
  • People in the household getting stomach or intestinal symptoms with no clear cause
  • Gurgling sounds from toilets or drains when other fixtures are used
  • Several fixtures backing up at once instead of only one
  • Toilet water with waste in it soaking into carpet or running across the floor
  • Strong sewage or rotten egg odors coming from drains or lower levels
  • Sewage or dark water backing up through floor drains, toilets or tubs
