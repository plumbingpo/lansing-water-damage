When to Call for Emergency Water Damage Restoration in Lansing

Recognizing the warning signs early can save you time, money, and prevent significant damage to your property. Watch for these telltale signs that professional help is needed:

  • The water is from a sewer backup or looks dark and smells bad.
  • Several hours or more have passed with hardwood, drywall or carpet sitting wet.
  • Trapped water overhead has made part of the ceiling bulge or sag.
  • Standing water is spreading across floors toward other rooms or down the stairs.
  • A water heater tank has failed and is emptying across the floor.
  • An outlet, a light fixture or the ceiling above has water dripping out of it.
  • Water from a floor above is streaming down interior walls.

How to Prevent Emergency Water Damage Restoration Problems

Taking preventive measures can help you avoid costly water damage issues down the road. Follow these tips from our IICRC-certified technicians:

  • Shut off the water at the main whenever you leave home for more than a few days.
  • Aging supply lines to toilets, faucets and washing machines should be swapped out before one fails.
  • A sump pump should be tried out before storms arrive, ideally with a backup battery installed.
  • Place leak sensors near the water heater, washing machine, dishwasher and under sinks.
  • Wrap plumbing in cold, unheated parts of the house so a hard freeze cannot crack it.
  • Every member of the household should know where the main water valve is and how it closes.
